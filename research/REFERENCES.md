# Research references and factual basis

1. **National Academic Depository / DigiLocker FAQ.** The official FAQ explains that awards are lodged by academic institutions; students retrieve and share them; employer/other verifier access is subject to student consent; and the verification response depends on the award having been published in the system.  
   https://nad.digilocker.gov.in/faq

2. **National Academic Depository verifier workflow.** The verifier process includes organisation registration through API Setu, obtaining student consent, entering identifying award details and receiving an NAD response.  
   https://nad.digilocker.gov.in/Verifier

3. **Press Information Bureau, Government of India. (7 July 2026). National Academic Depository.** Official backgrounder describing India's academic record-management context and the NAD/DigiLocker workflow.  
   https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2282200&lang=1&reg=3

4. **World Wide Web Consortium. (15 May 2025). Verifiable Credentials Data Model v2.0. W3C Recommendation.** Defines the issuer-holder-verifier ecosystem, issuer identification, credential status such as suspension/revocation, and privacy/data-minimisation considerations.  
   https://www.w3.org/TR/2025/REC-vc-data-model-2.0-20250515/

5. **Semiotic labour signals and socio-technical stratification: a meta-synthesis of micro-credentials in digital credentialing ecosystems. (Published online 5 August 2026).** Systematic review synthesising 86 studies; reports continuing concerns around standardisation, verifiability, assessment rigour, employer recognition and credential portability.  
   https://doi.org/10.1080/2331186X.2026.2698896

## Research-use note

The official NAD sources describe an existing Indian verification route; CertiChain must not be positioned as a replacement for it. The proposed EACT layer is a research hypothesis and prototype direction, not an existing NAD integration or an endorsed government workflow. The literature review summary is based on the publisher's abstract; obtain and read the full paper before submitting a final scholarly literature review.
