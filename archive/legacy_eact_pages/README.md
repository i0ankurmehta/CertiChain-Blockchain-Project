# Archived EACT Streamlit Pages

These five Streamlit pages were built for earlier EACT experiments around credential-ledger evidence triage, provenance freshness, trust-anchor lifecycle, privacy-preserving disclosure and SD-JWT.

They are preserved here for historical reproducibility and are intentionally removed from the active `pages/` navigation on the Trust Gatekeeper research branch.

They are not part of the Trust Gatekeeper BGV v1 product scope. Do not restore them to active navigation without a scope review against TG-RULES-001 and TG-APP-001.

The corresponding backend modules, tests and research notes remain in the repository for reference. The original `main` branch remains unchanged.

To compare or restore an experiment, refer to its archived source here and the original EACT protocol/results documents under `research/`.
